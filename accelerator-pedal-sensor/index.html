P2120 to P2138

Accelerator pedal sensor and throttle agreement

Fault codes P2120 to P2138 explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: HighThis can damage the engine or gearbox, or leave you stranded. Stop if the car feels unsafe, and have it checked now.

What it means

Cars with electronic throttle have no cable. Sensors on the pedal tell the computer what your foot is asking for. Two signals are compared all the time. If they disagree, the computer cannot be sure what you want and cuts power to stay safe.

Bank 1 is the side of the engine that has cylinder 1. Sensor 1 is before the catalytic converter and sensor 2 is after it. More terms explained.

What you may notice

  • Sudden loss of power, engine stuck near idle
  • Warning light for engine power or throttle
  • Accelerator feels dead or delayed

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • A failing pedal sensor
  • A failing throttle body
  • Water or corrosion in a connector
  • Chafed wiring between pedal, computer and throttle

What to check first

  • If power drops in traffic, switch on hazard lights and move off the road safely
  • Switching the engine off and on may restore power for a while, but the fault will return
  • Have it checked soon: this is a safety system

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.
