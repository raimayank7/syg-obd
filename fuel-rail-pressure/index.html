P0087, P0190 and related

Fuel rail pressure

Fault codes P0087, P0190 and related explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: HighThis can damage the engine or gearbox, or leave you stranded. Stop if the car feels unsafe, and have it checked now.

What it means

Fuel is held at pressure in a pipe called the rail, ready for the injectors. On direct-injection petrol engines and all modern diesels the pressure is very high. These codes mean the pressure is not what the computer asked for, or the sensor that measures it cannot be trusted.

CodeMeaning

P0194Fuel rail pressure sensor signal cuts in and out

Bank 1 is the side of the engine that has cylinder 1. Sensor 1 is before the catalytic converter and sensor 2 is after it. More terms explained.

What you may notice

  • Loss of power, most of all under load or uphill
  • Engine cuts out or will not start
  • Long cranking before starting
  • Limp mode

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • A blocked fuel filter (very common on diesels)
  • A weak low-pressure or high-pressure fuel pump
  • A faulty pressure regulator or control valve
  • A failed rail pressure sensor or its wiring
  • Running the tank very low or contaminated fuel

What to check first

  • Check when the fuel filter was last changed
  • Do not keep driving a diesel that loses power with this code: a failing pump can send metal through the whole fuel system
  • Never loosen high-pressure fuel pipes yourself: the pressure can cause serious injury

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.
