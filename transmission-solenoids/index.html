P0745 to P0778

Shift and pressure control solenoids

Fault codes P0745 to P0778 explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: MediumDrive gently and have it repaired soon. Leaving it can lead to bigger faults or higher fuel use.

What it means

Solenoids are small electric valves inside an automatic gearbox. Shift solenoids route fluid to select each gear. Pressure control solenoids set how firmly the gears engage. A code may mean an electrical fault in the solenoid, or that the gear change it controls did not happen as expected.

What you may notice

  • Harsh, soft or late gear changes
  • A gear missing
  • Gearbox stuck in second or third gear (limp mode)
  • Slipping

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • Dirty or low transmission fluid clogging the solenoid
  • A failed solenoid coil
  • A damaged internal wiring loom or connector
  • Valve body wear

What to check first

  • Check the fluid level and condition
  • Electrical codes (the ones ending in 3 or 8) usually mean the solenoid or wiring; "stuck" codes can be dirt or wear
  • Solenoids can often be replaced without removing the gearbox

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.
