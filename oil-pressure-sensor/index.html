P0520 to P0524

Engine oil pressure sensor

Fault codes P0520 to P0524 explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: HighThis can damage the engine or gearbox, or leave you stranded. Stop if the car feels unsafe, and have it checked now.

What it means

Oil pressure keeps the moving metal parts of the engine apart. A sensor reports it to the computer and the warning lamp. Most of these codes are about the sensor circuit, but P0524 means the pressure itself is too low. Treat any of them as real until proven otherwise.

Codes in this group

CodeMeaning
P0520Oil pressure sensor circuit fault
P0521Oil pressure reading out of range
P0522Oil pressure sensor signal low
P0523Oil pressure sensor signal high
P0524Engine oil pressure too low

Bank 1 is the side of the engine that has cylinder 1. Sensor 1 is before the catalytic converter and sensor 2 is after it. More terms explained.

What you may notice

  • Red oil warning light, steady or flickering
  • Ticking or knocking from the engine
  • Oil pressure gauge reading zero or maximum

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • Low oil level
  • A failed oil pressure sensor (common, and it often leaks)
  • The wrong oil grade or very old oil
  • A blocked oil pick-up or worn oil pump
  • Worn engine bearings

What to check first

  • Stop the engine as soon as it is safe if the red oil light is on
  • Check the oil level on the dipstick and top up if low
  • If the level is correct and the light stays on, do not drive: have the car towed and the real pressure measured

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.

Related codes

Engine overheating or overspeed (P0217), Fuel level sensor (P0460), Radiator cooling fan circuit (P0480), Vehicle speed sensor (P0500), Idle speed control (P0505), Brake pedal switch (P0504), Air conditioning pressure sensor (P0530), Battery and charging voltage (P0560). All codes.
