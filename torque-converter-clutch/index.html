P0740 to P0744

Torque converter clutch

Fault codes P0740 to P0744 explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: MediumDrive gently and have it repaired soon. Leaving it can lead to bigger faults or higher fuel use.

What it means

The torque converter joins the engine to an automatic gearbox through fluid. At steady speed a clutch inside it locks the two together to save fuel. These codes mean the clutch is not locking, is stuck locked, or its control solenoid has an electrical fault.

Codes in this group

CodeMeaning
P0740Torque converter clutch circuit fault

P0742Torque converter clutch stuck engaged
P0743Torque converter clutch electrical fault
P0744Torque converter clutch operation cuts in and out

Bank 1 is the side of the engine that has cylinder 1. Sensor 1 is before the catalytic converter and sensor 2 is after it. More terms explained.

What you may notice

  • Engine speed a little higher than usual at cruising speed
  • Shudder at 60 to 80 km/h
  • Engine stalling when you stop (clutch stuck on)
  • Higher fuel use

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • Old or wrong transmission fluid
  • A faulty lock-up solenoid
  • A worn torque converter
  • Valve body wear
  • Wiring fault

What to check first

  • Check when the transmission fluid was last changed and whether the correct type was used
  • A fluid change sometimes cures a shudder, but not a worn converter

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.
