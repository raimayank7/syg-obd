P0410 to P0419

Secondary air injection

Fault codes P0410 to P0419 explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: LowUsually safe to keep driving. Repair it when convenient, and do not ignore it for months.

What it means

On some petrol engines a small electric pump blows fresh air into the exhaust for about a minute after a cold start. The extra air helps the catalytic converter heat up quickly. These codes mean the pump, a valve or their wiring is not working.

Bank 1 is the side of the engine that has cylinder 1. Sensor 1 is before the catalytic converter and sensor 2 is after it. More terms explained.

What you may notice

  • Usually only the warning light
  • A missing or unusually loud whirring noise just after a cold start

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • A failed air pump, often after water got into it
  • A stuck or leaking check valve
  • A blown fuse or failed relay
  • Cracked air hoses

What to check first

  • Listen for a vacuum-cleaner sound for the first minute after a cold start: no sound suggests the pump or its fuse
  • The car is safe to drive, but may fail an emission test

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.

Related codes

Exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) (P0400), Catalytic converter efficiency (P0420), Fuel vapour (EVAP) system (P0440), Diesel particulate filter (DPF) (P2002), Diesel exhaust fluid (AdBlue) and SCR system (P203F). All codes.

Written in November 2026. These are generic OBD-II codes, the same on all makes. Causes and repair steps differ between models.
